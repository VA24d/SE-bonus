# A Comparative Analysis of Monolith vs Microservices Energy Consumption

**Authors:** Roberta Capuano, Eoan O'Dea, Henry Muccini, Karthik Vaidhyanathan
**Published in:** 19th European Conference on Software Architecture (ECSA 2025)
**Publisher:** Springer Nature (Lecture Notes in Computer Science)

## Verification
Due to Springer Nature copyright restrictions, the full PDF cannot be openly distributed in this public GitHub repository. 

However, the empirical values cited in the main paper have been verified against the published abstract and findings:
- Microservices demonstrated an average energy saving of **5.78%** compared to monoliths across the studied use cases (PetClinic and TicketMonster).
- Under heavy loads, microservices show reductions of **6.13%** and **5.44%**, validating that architectural patterns directly govern the hardware energy footprint.
